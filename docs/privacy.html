Hush mode and privacy

Your photos and settings stay on the frame's memory card. There's no account, no cloud storage and no app.

What the frame can send

Only two things, and only if you allow them:

  • The time from pool.ntp.org, the public time service. It sees that a device asked for the time.
  • Your time zone and rough location from ip-api.com. This lookup sees your home's public IP address, and it travels over plain HTTP, so anyone on the path could read it. It returns a time zone and a city-level location, which the frame uses for the sun and moon cards.

The frame sends nothing else: no usage data, no photos, no checks for updates.

Hush mode

With Hush on, the frame makes no connections to the internet at all. It still uses your home Wi-Fi so your phone can reach its page.

  • The page sets the clock whenever you open it, using your phone or computer's time. The clock chip keeps time in between.
  • Your browser's time zone is offered when you set up. You choose a US ZIP code, or enter latitude and longitude, for the sun and moon cards. Both are worked out on your own device and the frame, with no lookup.
  • Automatic setup and the internet time server setting are switched off and can't be turned on while Hush is on.

Until you answer the first-visit wizard, the frame behaves as if Hush were on, so a new frame never calls out before you've chosen. Switch Hush on or off any time in Settings → Time and place, or reopen the wizard with Run first-time setup again.

One honest note: the clock chip's drift hasn't been measured. In Hush mode, open the page now and then to keep the time right.

Who can reach the frame

The frame's page has no password. Anyone on your Wi-Fi, or within range of its own hotspot, can see and change photos and settings. Keep it on a network you trust. The page is protected against other websites sending it commands, and it only answers to the frame's own name or address.

The frame's own Wi-Fi is protected by a password made up for each frame. It's shown on the frame's screen.

The pages that link out

The frame's page has links to these guides and to GitHub. Nothing is contacted until you click, and the link doesn't tell the website which frame address you came from.
