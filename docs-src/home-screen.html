Add it to your home screen

Put the frame's page on your phone as an icon, so adding a photo is one tap.

Do this on your home Wi-Fi, with the frame awake (press KEY if its red light is off). Open the frame's page first, using the address from its screen, such as halcyon-c121.local.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep the name "Halcyon Frame", or rename it (handy if you have more than one frame), and tap Add.

Android

  1. Open the page in Chrome.
  2. Open the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm the name and tap Add.

The frame's page isn't served over a secure (https) connection, so Android adds a shortcut that opens full-screen without the address bar, rather than installing it as an app. It works the same for adding photos.

Good to know

  • The icon opens whichever address you added it from. If your router gives the frame a new IP address, an icon made from the IP address stops working. The .local name keeps working on iPhones, and on most Android phones. If it doesn't on yours, give the frame a fixed address in your router.
  • You must be on the same Wi-Fi as the frame. Away from home, the icon can't reach it.
  • If the frame is asleep, the icon shows a "can't connect" message. Press KEY on the frame, wait a few seconds, and open it again.
