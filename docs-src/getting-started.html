Getting started

From a frame in the box to your first photo on the wall, in about ten minutes.

What you need

  • A Waveshare ESP32-S3-PhotoPainter (the 7.3" six-colour frame). Halcyon Frame isn't affiliated with Waveshare.
  • A microSD (TF) card, formatted FAT32, in the frame's slot. Your photos and settings live on it.
  • A USB-C data cable (a charge-only cable won't work) and a computer with Chrome or Edge, for the install.
  • A phone or computer on your home Wi-Fi, for adding photos afterwards.

1. Install

Use the installer on the home page. It replaces the factory firmware, so if you might want it back, read Going back to the factory firmware first and make a backup.

2. Join the frame

When the frame restarts, it shows Hi! Let's add photos. with its own Wi-Fi name (like Halcyon-C121), a password, and two QR codes.

  1. Scan the first QR code with your phone's camera to join the frame's Wi-Fi, or choose the name from your Wi-Fi list and type the password from the screen.
  2. If your phone says the network has no internet and asks whether to stay connected, stay connected.
  3. Scan the second QR code, or open 192.168.4.1 in a browser.

The frame's page opens. The password is made up for each frame when it first starts and is shown on its screen. If the screen is blank or asleep, press KEY on the frame.

3. Answer the first question

The first time you open the page, a short wizard asks how the frame should behave:

  • Hush: the frame never connects to the internet. You choose the time zone and ZIP code once.
  • Let the frame look things up: it sets its own clock and works out your time zone and rough location.

You can change this later in Settings. What each choice sends.

4. Add your first photo

Drop a photo on the page or tap the box. An editor opens so you can choose how it fits the frame. Press Upload, and the photo appears on the frame about 20 seconds later. More about adding photos.

5. Put it on your home Wi-Fi

In Settings, pick your home network from the list, type its password, and press Save & connect. The frame restarts, joins your Wi-Fi and shows its new address, for example halcyon-c121.local, along with a QR code. Use that address from now on, on any phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi. If joining fails, the frame goes back to its own Wi-Fi and the screen says why.

You can also add the page to your phone's home screen.

Then

  • The frame sleeps to save power. After 10 minutes without anyone using the page it goes to sleep, and it shows your photos on its own schedule. Press KEY to wake it.
  • Choose how often photos change, and set quiet hours, in Settings.
